Learn practical French one focused journey at a time.

Paretto turns useful vocabulary into short, memorable lessons inspired by regions across France. Start without an account, learn at your own pace, and listen to clear packaged pronunciation even when your connection is unreliable.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Follow an 18-region journey with 54 focused lessons
• Learn 270 useful words and expressions
• Hear high-quality French pronunciation for every learning card
• Review only vocabulary you have already encountered
• Build a personal wordbook as you learn
• Practise with Château Challenge and Travel Dice
• Use reminders to keep a steady routine
• Continue learning offline with progress saved on your device

YOUR PROGRESS, YOUR CHOICE

No account is required to start learning. Continue with Apple only if you want secure account access and supported cross-device synchronization. You can export your learning data, sign out, or delete your account and learning data from Profile.

BUILT FOR ACCESS

Paretto supports iPhone and iPad layouts, Dynamic Type, reduced motion, semantic controls, and clear text labels alongside audio.

Paretto contains no advertising, subscriptions, or in-app purchases in this version.
